# Kernel and vendor

KompaktOS comes with its own `boot` and `vendor` images, built from Mudita's
published source with fixes on top. The update package installs them together
with the system. This page lists what they add.

## What our vendor adds

`vendor.img` carries the e-ink driver modules and the hardware permissions, so
most of what you notice comes from here.

- **Partial e-ink updates.** Only the part of the screen that changed is
  redrawn, not the whole screen every time, so it flashes far less and the
  quality modes are usable day to day.
- **Auto e-ink mode.** Fast while the screen moves, full quality once it
  settles. Set it per app in the Kompakt app.
- **E-ink settings that work.** The Kompakt app can change the refresh mode,
  manual mode, temperature offset and dithering.
- **A charge limit that is enforced.** Charging stops where you set it.
- **Fingerprint scrolling.** Swipe along the sensor to scroll. See
  [Fingerprint](/features/hardware/fingerprint).
- **Your own sleep screen.** The picture shown while the phone sleeps can be set
  from the Kompakt app. See
  [Sleep screen](/features/sleep-screen).

## What our boot adds

`boot.img` is the kernel, and with it the recovery.

- **Correct battery health.** The design capacity is reported as it is, about
  3000 mAh, so battery health reads right.
- **A real charge cycle count.** It shows in the Kompakt app and in Android's
  battery settings.
- **An always-on display that lets the phone sleep.** The display wakes
  cleanly from the always-on state, so the phone sleeps between clock updates:
  3–4% overnight. See
  [Always-on display](/features/aod).
- **A recovery that installs KompaktOS updates.** Our boot comes with LineageOS
  recovery, which installs a KompaktOS update from a single file, over USB or
  from the SD card.

## Always as a pair

Our `boot` and `vendor` are built and tested together, and every update package
installs both. The vendor comes in a Global and a North American version, to
match the phone's modem; see
[Editions](/install/editions#global-and-north-american-phones).

To go back to MuditaOS later you need Mudita's recovery again; see
[Going back](/install/going-back).
