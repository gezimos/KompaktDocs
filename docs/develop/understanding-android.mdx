# Understanding Android

The few things worth knowing before you [install](/install/install-kompaktos).

## Typing commands

- Commands are typed on your computer, with the phone connected by USB.
- Use Terminal on macOS, a terminal on Linux, or Command Prompt on Windows.
- Type the command after the prompt (`$`, `%` or `>`) and press Enter. Do not
  type the prompt itself.

![A terminal with adb reboot recovery typed after the prompt](/img/terminal.svg)

## The two files

- **The recovery** (`KompaktOS-Kernel-LineageOS-Recovery-1.0.img`): flashed
  once, from the bootloader.
- **The update package** (`KompaktOS-1.0-….zip`): installs KompaktOS from the
  recovery. Every update after that is a new package.

## Slots A and B

- The phone keeps two copies of the system, slot **A** and slot **B**.
- Each install or update goes into the other slot and switches to it.
- So running from B is normal. Do not switch slots yourself.

## The screens

### Bootloader

One line of text and nothing else. This is where the recovery is flashed. Get
there with `adb reboot bootloader`, or hold **Volume-Down** while plugging in USB.

### Recovery

Our LineageOS recovery installs KompaktOS and its updates, over USB or from an
SD card. Get there with `adb reboot recovery`.

### Boot mode menu

Hold **Power and Volume-Up**.

- Volume-Up moves the arrow, Volume-Down picks.
- The screen is slow here: wait a few seconds after each press.
