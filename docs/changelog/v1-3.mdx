# KompaktOS 1.3 beta

Not released yet. Everything that changes when you update from 1.2.

Each change has a number. If you have a question about one, mention its
number, for example "1.3 #14".

## Security

1. Android security update of 1 October 2026 (1.2 had 1 August 2026).
2. The SIM PIN is asked again when you start the phone with Offline+ on. Before,
   switching Offline+ off connected the SIM without its PIN.

## Google

3. Google accounts can be added again. After Google Play Services updated
   itself, the sign-in screen crashed.

## Lock screen and always-on display

4. Locking goes straight to the always-on display, with no sleep picture
   before it and no ghost of it under the clock.
5. A media card on the lock screen, like Katapult's music widget:
   - The track on top, the cover beside previous, play/pause, next and stop.
   - The same card replaces the media notification in the notifications shade.
   - To hide it on the lock screen: Settings → Sound & vibration → Media →
     **Show media on lock screen**.
6. Quick Settings can be kept off the lock screen: Settings → Display → Lock
   screen → **Quick settings on lock screen**. Notifications still open.
7. Per-app invert no longer turns the lock screen black. It turns off when the
   phone locks and comes back when you unlock into the app.

## Speed

8. The phone is usable straight after a restart. Apps that do not need to start
   at boot start when you first open them, or a couple of minutes later. Clock,
   calendar reminders, Google Play Services and emergency alerts still start at
   once.
9. Fewer apps start in the background after a restart. Calls, texts, alarms
   and reminders are not held back.
10. Apps are closed far less often. Idle apps are compressed in memory instead,
    and apps are closed only when the phone is really short of memory.
11. The memory card is scanned five minutes after boot, not while you open your
    first apps. New files on it appear after the scan.

## E-ink

12. Adaptive brightness is made for a frontlight:
    - Low in the dark, about a quarter of the slider in low light.
    - Less as the room gets brighter.
    - Off from about the light of a cloudy window.
13. **Quality** redraws faster, and cleans the whole screen half as often.
    **Text** works the same way, so a small change no longer flashes the text
    around it.
14. No more **Auto** mode. It ghosted badly after scrolling. A phone or app set
    to Auto now uses **Fast + dither**, and the Auto tile is gone.
15. Waking the phone keeps your e-ink mode. Before, the lock screen always drew
    in Fast.

## Kompakt app

16. New settings in ⋮ → Advanced:
    - **Fast speed**: from 9 steps down to 5. Fewer steps follow your finger
      sooner, with more ghosting. 8 and 9 give deeper blacks and fewer traces.
    - **Dither pattern** for Fast + dither: Normal, Pattern, Hybrid or Noise.
    - **Dither brightness**: lightens the dark greys of Pattern, Hybrid and Noise,
      so photos keep their shadows.
    - **Frame rate**: 60, 30, 15 or 10 frames a second, 15 by default. Apps draw
      less often, which saves battery the screen cannot show anyway. Swipes still
      feel the same. Restarts the phone. By Jackson Goode.
17. A dither pattern per app, in the app's e-ink settings under its mode.
18. Text stays sharp with the Pattern, Hybrid and Noise dithers, and is as bold
    as in Normal. Photos are still dithered.
19. Temperature offset, Fast speed and Dither pattern each have **Reset to
    default** in their info.
20. Holding a Kompakt tile in Quick Settings opens the Kompakt app.

## Buttons and gestures

21. Both flashlights at once:
    - A **Both flashlights** lock screen shortcut, beside Warm flashlight.
    - Kompakt app → Settings → **Power button light** → **Both**.
    - A **Both flashlights** action for the fingerprint sensor.
    - If either light is on, it turns both off.
22. The warm flashlight stays on when you switch the white one off. Before, both
    went off while the warm tile still showed on.

## Settings

23. Swiping back a page returns to the same page. Before, a setting could slide
    onto the next page. The same fix is in Contacts, Phone, Calendar, Music,
    Files and the Camera settings.
24. The arrow that opens a notification stands alone, without its pill, and the
    first line of a notification has more room.
25. The notification light colour is picked on one bar of named colours, with a
    slider below it from the colour to white.

## Sound

26. A car AUX cable no longer opens the voice assistant or rewinds the music.

## Apps

27. **Music**: follows LineageOS's latest version. Album and playlist pages have
    four outlined buttons: shuffle, add to queue, play next and play all.
28. **Clock**: the settings icon shows in light mode.
29. **Katapult 1.7**: Google's At a Glance weather shows again.
30. **inkOS 0.7.**
31. **inkOS** is fast straight after an update. Before, it ran slowly until the
    phone had spent a night on the charger.
