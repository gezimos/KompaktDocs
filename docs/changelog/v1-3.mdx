# KompaktOS 1.3 beta

Not released yet. Everything that changes when you update from 1.2.

Each change has a number. If you have a question about one, mention its
number, for example "1.3 #14".

## New in this beta

Changed since the last beta (build 438). Each one is described in full under
its number below.

- Starting with Offline+ on no longer drains the battery (#4).
- Data usage is counted per app again (#5).
- The emergency dialer follows dark mode (#6).
- Apps you leave are frozen, and QcRilAm is gone (#16, #17).
- The frontlight learns from the brightness slider (#25).
- Changes of light fade in steps too small to see (#26).
- Auto brightness answers in about half a second (#27).
- **Auto refresh** flashes the moment you switch apps (#28).
- The Kompakt app has a new look: one row per setting (#29).
- Camera: the frame matches the photo or video, photos are always full size,
  and video stabilization is off (#41 to #44).
- Latest builds of Katapult 1.7 and inkOS 0.7 (#47, #48).

## Security

1. Android security update of 1 October 2026 (1.2 had 1 August 2026).
2. The SIM PIN is asked again when you start the phone with Offline+ on. Before,
   switching Offline+ off connected the SIM without its PIN.

## Google

3. Google accounts can be added again. After Google Play Services updated
   itself, the sign-in screen crashed.

## Phone and data

4. Starting the phone with Offline+ on no longer drains the battery.
   - Before, the modem tried to start every 14 seconds for as long as Offline+
     stayed on.
   - Now the modem starts only when you switch Offline+ off, and the network is
     back within seconds.
5. Data usage is counted per app again. Settings listed only "Hotspot &
   tethering".
6. The emergency dialer follows dark mode:
   - White in light mode, black in dark mode. Before, a dark lock screen
     wallpaper made it black in light mode.
   - An outlined call button instead of the green one.
   - The emergency information box is outlined, with no grey fill, and takes
     less height.

## Lock screen and always-on display

7. Locking goes straight to the always-on display, with no sleep picture
   before it and no ghost of it under the clock.
8. A media card on the lock screen, like Katapult's music widget:
   - The track on top, the cover beside previous, play/pause, next and stop.
   - The same card replaces the media notification in the notifications shade.
   - To hide it on the lock screen: Settings → Sound & vibration → Media →
     **Show media on lock screen**.
9. Quick Settings can be kept off the lock screen: Settings → Display → Lock
   screen → **Quick settings on lock screen**. Notifications still open.
10. Per-app invert no longer turns the lock screen black. It turns off when the
    phone locks and comes back when you unlock into the app.
11. Always-on display stability improvements:
    - It stays on when an app is inverted. Before, the phone stopped at the
      sleep picture.
    - A sleeping phone no longer keeps the lock screen drawn on the always-on
      display.
12. The always-on display repaints as often as you choose: Kompakt app →
    Display → **Always-on display refresh**.
    - Auto, 1, 5, 10 or 60 minutes, on the clock: 5 minutes repaints at 6:05,
      6:10.
    - Auto is as before: the clock every minute, and a notification at once.
    - With a number, a notification waits for the next repaint too.
    - Each repaint wakes the phone for about 2 seconds, so fewer repaints use
      less battery.

## Speed

13. Memory improvements at boot: after a restart the phone no longer starts all
    its apps at once, so it is usable sooner.
14. Fewer apps start in the background after a restart. Calls, texts, alarms
    and reminders are not held back.
15. Apps are closed far less often. Idle apps are compressed in memory instead,
    and apps are closed only when the phone is really short of memory.
16. Apps you leave are frozen:
    - A few seconds after you leave an app, it uses no processor until you come
      back.
    - Its memory is compressed, so more apps stay open.
17. One less app running for nothing: QcRilAm, a Qualcomm leftover that does
    nothing on this phone, is gone.
18. The memory card is scanned five minutes after boot, not while you open your
    first apps. New files on it appear after the scan.

## E-ink

19. Adaptive brightness is made for a frontlight:
    - Low in the dark, about a quarter of the slider in low light.
    - Less as the room gets brighter.
    - Off from about the light of a cloudy window.
20. **Quality** redraws faster, and cleans the whole screen half as often.
    **Text** works the same way, so a small change no longer flashes the text
    around it.
21. No more **Auto** mode. It ghosted badly after scrolling. A phone or app set
    to Auto now uses **Fast + dither**, and the Auto tile is gone.
22. Waking the phone keeps your e-ink mode. Before, the lock screen always drew
    in Fast.
23. A mode, Fast speed or temperature offset changed in the Kompakt app gets a
    black refresh, as a mode picked on a tile does.

## Display

24. Set the brightness curve yourself: Kompakt app → Display → Sleep screen &
    frontlight → **Auto brightness curve**.
    - The light, as a percent of full, for each kind of light: Dark, Dim room,
      Lamp light, Lit room, Bright room and Daylight.
    - The row in bold is the light you are in now, and its number is the light
      you get.
    - Each tap is one step you can see.
    - It is kept after a restart.
25. The frontlight learns from you:
    - With auto brightness on, move the brightness slider to the light you want.
    - From then on, in that much light, the frontlight comes up at that level by
      itself.
    - The curve page shows what it has learned; **Reset** forgets it.
26. A change of room fades, it does not jump:
    - The frontlight dims in steps too small to see, sixteen times finer at the
      dim end.
    - A change of light takes about a quarter of a second; lamp light to off
      takes about three.
    - The slider still answers at once.
27. Auto brightness answers in about half a second. Before, it waited 3 to 4
    seconds, and small changes of light were ignored.

## Kompakt app

28. New settings in ⋮ → Advanced:
    - **Fast speed**: from 9 steps down to 5. Fewer steps follow your finger
      sooner, with more ghosting. 8 and 9 give deeper blacks and fewer traces.
    - **Dither pattern** for Fast + dither: Normal, Pattern, Hybrid or Noise.
    - **Dither brightness**: lightens the dark greys of Pattern, Hybrid and Noise,
      so photos keep their shadows.
    - **Frame rate**: 60, 30, 15 or 10 frames a second, 60 by default. A lower
      rate makes apps draw less often, which saves battery the screen cannot show anyway. Swipes still
      feel the same. Restarts the phone. By Jackson Goode.
    - **Updates per full refresh**: 6, 12, 24 or 48 small updates before the whole page is
      redrawn once. 24 by default. A lower number clears ghosts sooner in Text
      and Quality.
    - **Auto refresh**: one flash the moment you open an app, switch to one or go
      back to Home, to clear what the last one left. Off by default.
29. A new look: every setting is one row.
    - An icon, the name, one line saying what it does, and its switch or value
      on the right.
    - Only what you pick or press has an outline: the mode circles, chips and
      buttons.
    - Longer explanations are behind the info icon.
    - The per-app list shows each app's icon with its mode under the name.
30. A dither pattern per app, in the app's e-ink settings under its mode.
31. Text stays sharp with the Pattern, Hybrid and Noise dithers, and is as bold
    as in Normal. Photos are still dithered.
32. Temperature offset, Fast speed and Dither pattern each have **Reset to
    default** in their info.
33. Holding a Kompakt tile in Quick Settings opens the Kompakt app.

## Buttons and gestures

34. Both flashlights at once:
    - A **Both flashlights** lock screen shortcut, beside Warm flashlight.
    - Kompakt app → Settings → **Power button light** → **Both**.
    - A **Both flashlights** action for the fingerprint sensor.
    - If either light is on, it turns both off.
35. The warm flashlight stays on when you switch the white one off. Before, both
    went off while the warm tile still showed on.

## Settings

36. Swiping back a page returns to the same page. Before, a setting could slide
    onto the next page. The same fix is in Contacts, Phone, Calendar, Music,
    Files and the Camera settings.
37. The arrow that opens a notification stands alone, without its pill, and the
    first line of a notification has more room.
38. The notification light colour is picked on one bar of named colours, with a
    slider below it from the colour to white.

## Recents

39. **No recent items** shows only on its white background, never over the home
    screen before it.

## Sound

40. A car AUX cable no longer opens the voice assistant or rewinds the music.

## Camera

41. The frame on screen matches the photo or video. In 720p and 1080p video the
    screen showed more above and below than the video kept.
42. Photos are always full size, 4:3 at 3264×2448:
    - A 16:9 photo was only 1920×1088 (2 MP).
    - The aspect ratio button is gone.
43. Video stabilization is off, and its switch is gone:
    - The camera has no optical stabilization (OIS).
    - The processor cannot run electronic stabilization (EIS) without dropping
      frames, so videos with it jumped.
44. The recording timer redraws once a second instead of about 16 times, which
    saves screen refreshes while you record.

## Apps

45. **Music**: follows LineageOS's latest version. Album and playlist pages have
    four outlined buttons: shuffle, add to queue, play next and play all.
46. **Clock**: the settings icon shows in light mode.
47. **Katapult 1.7**: Google's At a Glance weather shows again.
48. **inkOS 0.7.**
49. **inkOS** runs fast right after an update.

## Also fixed

50. The e-ink mode of each app is applied more reliably when you switch apps,
    and no longer needs an accessibility service.
51. Animations are off and stay off:
    - **Remove animations** is always on, and its switch is gone.
    - The animation settings in Developer options are hidden.
    - Turning Developer options off no longer turns animations back on.
52. The system no longer crashes when a heads-up notification appears.
53. Tapping outside the numbers no longer closes the PIN pad.
