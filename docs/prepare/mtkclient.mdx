# mtkclient

[mtkclient](https://github.com/bkerler/mtkclient) makes a full backup of your
phone over USB.

- **Optional, but strongly recommended**, even if you never install KompaktOS.
- If anything ever goes wrong, it puts your phone back as it was.
- You only do it once.

## Install

Follow mtkclient's own guide for your computer:

- **macOS and Linux:** [installation guide](https://github.com/bkerler/mtkclient/blob/main/README-INSTALL.md)
- **Windows:** [installation guide](https://github.com/bkerler/mtkclient/blob/main/README-WINDOWS.md),
  or the [automated Windows installer](https://github.com/codefl0w/mtkclient-windows-installer)
- **Using it:** [usage guide](https://github.com/bkerler/mtkclient/blob/main/README-USAGE.md)

## Back up

1. Start the mtkclient window (`mtk_gui`), as its guide describes.
2. Turn off the phone and unplug it.
3. Hold **Volume-Up and Volume-Down** while you plug in USB. Keep holding until
   mtkclient shows the phone.
4. **Read partition(s)** tab: tick **Select all partitions**, click
   **Read partition(s)** and pick an empty folder.

**Optional:** untick **userdata** to save hours. It is most of the storage, it
is encrypted, and installing formats it anyway.

- Keep that folder safe.
- Use nothing else in mtkclient: **Erase** and **Flash Tools** can break the
  phone for good.

If the phone ever stops starting, [Last resort](/develop/last-resort) puts
it back from this backup.
