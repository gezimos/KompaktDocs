# Requirements

- **A computer with adb and fastboot:** see
  [Install ADB](/prepare/unlocking#1-install-adb).
- **An unlocked bootloader:** see [Unlocking](/prepare/unlocking).
- **A backup:** installing erases the phone. See [Backup with mtkclient](/prepare/mtkclient).
- **MuditaOS 1.6.1:** update MuditaOS to 1.6.1 before installing.
- **Your modem version, checked on MuditaOS 1.6.1:**
  - With adb: `adb shell getprop gsm.version.baseband`
  - Or in Settings, under About phone: Baseband version.
  - `V236.P8` is a Global phone, `V236.P6` a North American one. See
    [Editions](/install/editions#global-and-north-american-phones).
- **The files for your phone and edition:** see
  [Install](/install/install-kompaktos#download-recovery).

**Relocking is only possible with MuditaOS-K installed. Do not try to relock
with KompaktOS.**
