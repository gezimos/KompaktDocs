# E-ink

All the e-ink settings are in the Kompakt app, under **Display**.

## Modes

<ScreenshotRow>
  <Screenshot src="/img/screens/eink-display-tab.png" caption="Display tab: the modes as circles, the current one marked and named in the heading." />
  <Screenshot src="/img/screens/eink-modes-info.png" caption="The (i) beside the heading explains each mode." />
</ScreenshotRow>

| Mode | What it does | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fast** | One-bit, no blink. Very high contrast; dark colours crush to black | Scrolling, lists |
| **Fast, soft** | Same speed, less contrast, so dark colours keep detail | Dark apps |
| **Fast + dither** | Two inks with dots of grey, no blink | Photos and gradients at speed |
| **Text** | Sharp greys, slower | Reading |
| **Quality** | The cleanest picture, slowest, blinks | PDFs, comics, photos |

- **Text** and **Quality** redraw only the pixels that change.
- They redraw the whole screen once every 24 small updates: see
  [Partial updates](#partial-updates).

The **e-ink** tile in Quick Settings switches the mode without opening the app:
each tap moves to the next one.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/eink-qs-tile.png" caption="The e-ink tile (circled), showing Fast + dither." />

## Per-app modes

An app gets its own mode while it is open. When you leave it, the mode set on
the Display tab comes back.

1. Display → **Per-app e-ink modes** lists every app with the mode it gets.
2. Tap an app and pick its mode.
3. **Follow master mode** gives it back the Display tab's mode.
4. For a mode with dots, such as **Fast + dither**, **Dither pattern** picks
   the app's own pattern. **Default** follows [Dither pattern](#dither-pattern).

For example: **Text** for a reader, **Fast** for Signal or WhatsApp.

## Clearing ghosting

The faster modes leave faint traces of earlier screens behind. A refresh flashes
the whole screen once, which clears them:

- **Long-press the home button.** Works from anywhere.
- The **Refresh** tile in Quick Settings.
- A fingerprint gesture, if you set one to **Refresh the screen**: see
  [Fingerprint](hardware/fingerprint).
- **Refresh on unlock** (Display tab): one refresh right after you unlock,
  from the always-on display, the sleep screen or the lock screen. Off for all
  three out of the box.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/eink-refresh-on-unlock.png" caption="Refresh on unlock: pick where the unlock comes from." />

### Fast mode speed

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/eink-fast-speed-info.png" caption="The (i) on Fast mode speed." />

- Choose 11, 9, 8, 7, 6 or 5. 11 is the default, Mudita's.
- The number is how many times the panel pushes the ink for each change in the
  Fast modes.
- Fewer pushes follow your finger sooner when you scroll, but leave more grey
  behind.
- Text and Quality are not affected; use Text / Quality speed for those.

### Text / Quality speed

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/eink-text-speed-info.png" caption="The (i) on Text / Quality speed." />

- Choose 46, 38, 33 or 28. 38 is the default.
- The number is how many times the panel pushes the ink for each change in Text
  and Quality.
- Fewer pushes redraw sooner and can leave a little more of the last page
  behind.
- The Fast modes are not affected.

### Dither pattern

**Fast + dither** turns greys into black and white dots. **Dither pattern**
decides where the dots go.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/eink-pattern-info.png" caption="Tap Dither pattern to choose how the dots go." />

| Pattern | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Normal** | Mudita's, the default. Dots that crawl when the picture moves |
| **Pattern** | A fine grid of dots that stays still |
| **Hybrid** | Dots guided by the grid: more detail than Pattern |
| **Noise** | Still like Pattern, without the grid |

With Pattern, Hybrid and Noise:

- Text, lines and icons stay sharp, and as bold as with Normal.
- Very light greys become plain white and very dark greys plain black, so
  backgrounds and text have no dots.

An app can have its own pattern: see [Per-app modes](#per-app-modes).

### Dither brightness

- Choose 0, +1, +2 or +3. 0 is the default.
- It lightens the dark greys, so photos keep the detail in their shadows
  instead of turning into solid black dots.
- Black, white and text don't change.
- Shown only while the pattern is Pattern, Hybrid or Noise.

### Partial updates

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/eink-partial-updates.png" caption="Partial updates: how many before a full refresh." />

- Choose 6, 12, 24 or 48 updates. 24 is the default.
- Text and Quality redraw only the part of the screen that changed. Each of
  these partial updates leaves a faint trace.
- After this many of them the whole screen gets a full refresh, with one blink.
- Fewer updates: the cleanest picture, with the most blinks. More updates:
  fewer blinks and more traces.
- The Fast modes never do this; they need a flash: see
  [Clearing ghosting](#clearing-ghosting).

### Frame rate

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/eink-frame-rate.png" caption="Frame rate: 60 fps is the default." />

- Choose 60, 30, 15 or 10 fps. 60 is the default.
- It caps how many frames per second every app draws: the work the processor
  and graphics chip do to make each new picture.
- It does not change the display. The e-ink panel cannot show 60 changes a
  second anyway, so frames above what it shows are wasted battery.
- Touch is still read at the full rate. Only drawing is capped, so taps and
  swipes land the same.
- Scrolling and animations update less often at a low rate.
- The phone restarts to apply a change.
