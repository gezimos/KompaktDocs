# E-ink

An e-ink panel holds its image without power, and every repaint takes time and
battery. The mode decides how each repaint is done: fast and rough, or slow and
clean.

The Kompakt app has the controls, under **Display**.

## Modes

  <Screenshot src="/img/screens/eink-modes-info.png" caption="The (i) beside the heading explains each mode." />
</ScreenshotRow>

| Mode | What it does | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fast** | One-bit, no blink. Very high contrast; dark colours crush to black | Scrolling, lists |
| **Fast, soft** | Same speed, less contrast, so dark colours keep detail | Dark apps |
| **Fast + dither** | Two inks with dots of grey, no blink | Photos and gradients at speed |
| **Text** | Sharp greys, slower | Reading |
| **Quality** | The cleanest picture, slowest, blinks | PDFs, comics, photos |
| **Auto** | Fast while the screen moves, quality when it settles | Leaving it alone |

The **e-ink** tile in Quick Settings switches the mode without opening the app:
each tap moves to the next one.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/eink-qs-tile.png" caption="The e-ink tile in Quick Settings, showing Fast + dither." />

## Clearing ghosting

The faster modes leave faint traces of earlier screens behind. A refresh flashes
the whole screen once, which clears them:

- **Long-press the home button.** Works from anywhere.
- The **Refresh** tile in Quick Settings.
- A fingerprint gesture, if you set one to **Refresh the screen**: see
  [Fingerprint](hardware/fingerprint).

## Per-app modes

An app can have its own mode, and dark-only apps can have their colours
inverted. See [Per-app modes](per-app).

## Temperature offset

Display → Temperature offset, the row of chips under the mode circles. The panel picks its waveform partly from
temperature, and claiming a warmer panel makes it use fewer waveform frames —
faster, at the cost of how cleanly the image settles.

## Why everything is black and white

The panel shows greys badly: in-between shades turn into a speckled pattern, and
see-through or moving effects flicker. So KompaktOS draws its screens in black
and white, and animations are turned off.
