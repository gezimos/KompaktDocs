# Kompakt features FAQ

Everything KompaktOS adds for the Kompakt, as short answers. Most of it lives
in the **Kompakt** app, in the app drawer, which has four tabs:

| Tab | What is there |
| --- | --- |
| **Display** | E-ink mode, per-app modes, refresh on unlock, sleep screen picture |
| **Offline** | What the offline switch turns off |
| **Style** | Font, emoji, display width |
| **Settings** | Fingerprint sensor, key vibration, Recents key, battery |

The **⋮** menu holds **Advanced**: temperature offset and diagnostics.

## The screen

### What is ghosting?

- Faint traces of earlier screens left on the panel.
- The faster [e-ink modes](eink#modes) leave more of it; a full refresh clears
  it.

### How do I clear ghosting by hand?

Any of these flashes the whole screen once:

- **Long-press the home button.** Works from anywhere.
- The **Refresh** tile in Quick Settings.
- **Double tap the fingerprint sensor**, once
  [fingerprint gestures](hardware/fingerprint#fingerprint-gestures) are on
  (Refresh the screen is the double tap out of the box).

### Can it clear ghosting when I unlock?

- Kompakt app → **Display** → **Refresh on unlock**.
- Choose where the unlock comes from, any or all of:
  - **From the always-on display**
  - **From the sleep screen**
  - **From the lock screen**
- The screen refreshes once, right after the unlocked screen is drawn.
- Off for all three out of the box.

### Does locking the phone clear the screen?

- Yes. Every lock clears the panel with a white and black flash, as on
  MuditaOS.

### Which e-ink mode should I use?

| Mode | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Fast** | Scrolling and lists. Very high contrast; dark colours go black |
| **Fast, soft** | Dark apps. Same speed, keeps detail in dark colours |
| **Fast + dither** | Photos and gradients at speed. Dots of grey, no blink |
| **Text** | Reading. Sharp greys, slower |
| **Quality** | PDFs, comics, photos. Cleanest, slowest, blinks |
| **Auto** | Leaving it alone. Fast while the screen moves, quality when it settles |

- Set it in Kompakt app → **Display**, or tap the **E-ink** tile in Quick
  Settings to step through the modes.
- Each mode also has its own Quick Settings tile (**E-ink: Fast**,
  **E-ink: Text**…): a tap switches straight to it. Add them in the Quick
  Settings editor.
- More in [E-ink](eink).

### Can one app have its own mode?

- Yes. Display → **Per-app e-ink modes** → pick an app → pick its mode.
- The app gets its mode when it opens; the Display tab's mode comes back when
  you leave it.
- **Follow master mode** gives it back the Display tab's mode.
- Apps that are always dark, such as Spotify, can be inverted to black on
  white: **Invert colours** on the same sheet.
- More in [Per-app modes](per-app).

### The picture is not clean enough, or redraws too slowly

- Kompakt app → **⋮** → **Advanced** → **Temperature offset**.
- A higher offset tells the panel it is warmer than it is: faster redraws,
  slightly less clean.
- Back to 0 for the cleanest picture.

### Why is everything black and white, with no animations?

- In-between greys turn into a speckled pattern on the panel.
- See-through and moving effects flicker and cost a repaint each frame.
- So KompaktOS draws its screens in two inks, black and white, and animations
  are off.
- Lists turn a page per swipe instead of scrolling with your finger: one
  repaint per page instead of dozens.

### Can I fit more on the screen?

- Kompakt app → **Style** → **Display width**: **360**, **400** or **440**.
- Wider fits more, with smaller text and controls. 360 is the default.
- The **Width** tile in Quick Settings steps through them.

## Lock screen and sleep

### Can I use my own sleep screen picture?

- Kompakt app → Display → **Sleep screen picture** → **Choose picture**.
- **Fit** shows all of it on white; **Fill** fills the screen and crops the
  edges.
- **Default** brings back the built-in picture.
- More in [Sleep screen](sleep-screen).

### What does the always-on display cost?

- About **3–4%** of the battery overnight.
- The phone sleeps and wakes once a minute to paint the clock.
- Shows the clock, notification icons and the battery level.
- Turn it on or off with the **Always on display** tile in Quick Settings.
- More in [Always-on display](aod).

## Text

### Can I change the font?

- Kompakt app → **Style** → **Font**.
- Google Sans (the default), Roboto, Manrope, Noto Serif, Source Serif, Lato or
  JetBrains Mono.
- Each name in the list is shown in its own font.
- It changes the text of every app that does not bring its own font, headings
  and the clock included.
- The phone restarts to apply a change.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/kompakt-font.png" caption="Style → Font, each name in its own font." />

### Why is the Font option missing, or Google Sans not offered?

- It depends on your phone's language:
  - **Latin alphabets** (English, German, Polish, Turkish, Vietnamese…): every
    font.
  - **Greek and Cyrillic**: every font but Google Sans, which has no Greek or
    Cyrillic letters. Roboto is the default.
  - **Other scripts** (Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hebrew, Hindi,
    Thai…): no Font row. None of the fonts has these letters, so Android's own
    fonts draw them, with Roboto for Latin letters and digits.
- A language change applies to the font at the next restart.

### Can I have colour emoji?

- Kompakt app → **Style** → **Emoji**: **Black and white** or **Colour**.
- Black and white is the default: crisp on e-ink, in the text colour.
- Flags and the newest emoji, which the black and white font does not have,
  stay in colour.
- The phone restarts to apply a change.

## Buttons and sensors

### What do the keys under the screen do?

- **Home**: a tap goes home; a long press refreshes the screen.
- **Recents**: a tap opens recent apps. With **Hold Recents for quick
  settings** on (Kompakt app → Settings), a long press opens quick settings.
- **Vibrate the keys under the screen** (Kompakt app → Settings) turns their
  buzz on or off.

### Can the fingerprint sensor do more than unlock?

- Yes, while the phone is unlocked. Kompakt app → **Settings** → **Fingerprint
  sensor** → **Use while unlocked**.
- Swipe along it to scroll, move a D-pad, change the front light or press a key
  for Key Mapper.
- Double tap, triple tap and hold each get an action: refresh the screen,
  switch mode, open notifications or quick settings, turn the front light on
  or off, the warm or cold flashlight, and more.
- More in [Fingerprint](hardware/fingerprint).

### Can I turn on the warm flashlight from the lock screen?

- Yes. Settings → **Wallpaper & style** → **Lock screen** → **Shortcuts**, and
  pick **Warm flashlight** for one side.
- **Flashlight** is the cold light; you can put one on each side.
- The warm light is also a Quick Settings tile, **Warm light**, and a
  fingerprint action.

### Which light does a long press of the power button turn on?

- The cold one, out of the box.
- Kompakt app → **Settings** → **Power button light** → **Warm** switches it to
  the warm one.
- The long press works with the screen off. Turn it on in Settings →
  **System** → **Buttons** → **Power button** → **Long press for torch**.
- With the light on, a long press turns it off again, screen on or off.

### What does the offline switch turn off?

- Always: the mobile network and the microphone, in hardware.
- Your choice: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and camera access. Kompakt app → **Offline**.
- A panel shows which way the switch now is.
- More in [Offline switch](hardware/offline-switch).

### Can the notification light have a colour per app?

- Yes. Settings → **Notifications** → **Notification light** → **Apps** →
  **Add**.
- More in [Notification light](hardware/notification-light).

## Battery

### Can I stop charging at 80%?

- Kompakt app → **Settings** → **Battery** → **Charge limit**: anywhere from 80
  to 100.
- Charging stops at the limit and resumes a little below it.
- More in [Battery](battery#charge-limit).

### Where are battery cycles and health?

- Kompakt app → Settings → **Battery**: cycles, capacity, health and the date
  the battery was first used.
- The cycle count is the real measure of wear. More in
  [Battery](battery#cycles-capacity-and-health).

## Camera

### Can photos and videos have a film look?

- In the camera, open the options, the row with 4:3, the timer and the grid.
  **Style** is the first option.
- Photos: **Normal**, **B&W** or **Film** (colour film grain).
- Videos: **Normal**, **B&W** or **VHS**.
  - VHS records at SD and comes out 4:3, with the camcorder clock in the
    corner.
  - B&W video keeps the resolution you recorded at.
- The style is applied once the photo or video is saved, in the background. A
  video takes a while; a message says when it is done.
- If a style cannot be applied, the original is kept.
- While a style is on, its icon shows at the top of the viewfinder.

### Can the camera save to the SD card?

- Yes. In the camera's settings, turn on **Save to SD card**. The switch only
  shows while an SD card is in.
- Photos and videos go to the card's DCIM/Camera folder.
- With no card in, they go to the phone, so nothing is lost.
- A card set up as internal storage is already the phone's storage, so the
  switch does not apply to it. USB drives are never used.

### Why does the camera call the back camera "Front"?

- The Kompakt has one camera, on the back.
- Some banking and ID apps refuse to continue without a front camera, so the
  back camera is offered as both.
- More in [Camera](hardware/camera#front-camera).

## Something is not right

### Where can I see what is installed?

- Kompakt app → **⋮** → **Advanced** → **Diagnostics**.
- Shows which system, boot and vendor images are installed, calls over 4G, the
  notification light and the e-ink panel's values.
- Useful to include when you [ask for help](/support).

### A Kompakt app setting says it needs our boot or vendor image

- Some features need KompaktOS's own boot or vendor image, not Mudita's.
- The full update package installs both. See [Updates](/install/updates).
