# Always-on display

- The always-on display shows the clock, notification icons and the battery
  level.
- A few notification icons show; the rest collapse into one dot.
- Waking the phone shows the lock screen with the notifications in full.

## Turning it on or off

Use the **Always on display** tile in Quick Settings.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/aod-qs-tile.png" caption="The always-on display tile (circled), on." />

## Wallpaper

- The always-on display and the lock screen share one wallpaper: the lock
  screen wallpaper.
- Change it in Settings → **Wallpaper & style**, on the lock screen preview.
- It is not the [sleep screen picture](sleep-screen), which shows only while the
  always-on display is off.

## Battery

- The phone wakes only to update the clock and show new notifications, for
  about 2 seconds, then goes back to deep sleep (doze).
- The e-ink screen keeps the clock without power while the phone sleeps.
- So it uses far less battery than a screensaver, which keeps the phone awake
  the whole time.
- Fewer clock updates use less still: see
  [How often the clock updates](#how-often-the-clock-updates).

## How often the clock updates

In the Kompakt app: Display → **Always-on display refresh**.

- **Auto** (the default): the clock every minute, notifications at once.
- **1, 5, 10 or 60 min**: the clock and notifications update together at that
  interval. At 5 min the clock shows 6:05, then 6:10.
- Fewer updates, more battery.
