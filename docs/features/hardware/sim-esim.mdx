# SIM and eSIM

Two SIMs work at once. SIM 1 is always the tray. Slot 2 is either the second
tray or the built-in eSIM, one at a time: the two share one slot in hardware.

Everything is under Settings → **Network & internet** → **SIMs**:

## Setting slot 2 to eSIM

1. If a physical SIM is in the second tray, turn it off first (its **Use this
   SIM** switch). Otherwise the change is refused.
2. Turn on **SIM 2 slot as eSIM**. The phone restarts.

The **eSIM** tile in Quick Settings does the same. To go back to the tray, turn
the switch off again; the phone restarts again.

## Adding an eSIM

The eSIM is managed by **OpenEUICC**, an open-source eSIM manager that comes with
KompaktOS. It downloads an eSIM from the QR code you were given, and switches
between eSIMs: the chip can hold several, with one in use at a time.

You can add one from either place; both use OpenEUICC:

- Settings → **SIMs** → **Add SIM** → **Download eSIM**
- the **OpenEUICC** app → **+**

Set slot 2 to eSIM first, as above. When asked which eSIM to download to, pick
the eSIM chip. It shows as **Logical Slot 1**, because the slots are counted
from 0. Then scan the QR code, load it from a picture or the clipboard, or type
it in.

## A SIM's own settings

Tap a SIM in the list for its settings: data usage, roaming, the phone number,
VoLTE, the preferred network type and the access point names.
