# Camera

The Kompakt has one camera, on the back.

## Front camera

Some apps, such as some banking and ID-check apps, refuse to continue on a
phone without a front camera.

- KompaktOS offers the rear camera as the front camera too, so these apps carry
  on.
- When one asks for a selfie, turn the phone round and point the back camera at
  yourself.
- The camera app's switch button works too: it swaps between **Back** and
  **Front**, which is the same camera.
