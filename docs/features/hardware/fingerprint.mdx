# Fingerprint reader

Works. The sensor is in the power button on the side, and the interface is told
so — otherwise Android draws the unlock prompt in the wrong place.

Add a fingerprint in Settings → **Security & privacy** → **Device unlock**.
**Touch to unlock anytime** unlocks with a touch even while the screen is off,
without pressing the button.

## Fingerprint gestures

The sensor also works as a small trackpad and a button: swipe your thumb along
it, tap it two or three times, or rest a finger on it. It works while the
phone is unlocked; on the lock screen the sensor unlocks as usual.

Kompakt app → **Settings** (the gear) → **Fingerprint sensor**, then turn on
**Use while unlocked**.

### Swipe modes

What a swipe does depends on the mode:

| Mode | A swipe |
| --- | --- |
| **Scroll** | scrolls the screen, like a thumb drag; a long swipe goes further |
| **D-pad** | presses the arrow keys, for apps you move through by focus |
| **Brightness** | turns the front light up or down |
| **Keymapper** | presses a key for the [Key Mapper](https://github.com/keymapperorg/KeyMapper) app: F13 for a swipe up, F14 for a swipe down |
| **Nothing** | does nothing; the taps and the hold still work |

**Modes** opens one sheet with all of them. Drag a mode by its ≡ handle to
reorder, and switch each one on or off. **Switch mode** goes through the modes
that are on, in that order. The first one on is the default, and the phone
starts in it each time the screen wakes. Keymapper is off until you turn it on.

**Natural scrolling** and **Sideways, for page turns** set the direction for
Scroll and D-pad.

### Taps and hold

| Gesture | Out of the box |
| --- | --- |
| **Double tap** | **Refresh the screen** |
| **Triple tap** | **Switch mode**, with a message saying which |
| **Hold** (a finger still for about a second) | **Enter**: presses what the D-pad is on |

Each can instead do nothing, switch mode, press Enter, open recent apps,
refresh the screen, open notifications or quick settings, toggle the warm or
cold flashlight, switch between vertical and horizontal, or press a key for
Key Mapper: double tap is the red key, triple tap the yellow key and hold the
green key. A hold sent to Key Mapper stays pressed until you lift your finger,
so Key Mapper can tell a long press.

With a triple tap set to anything but Nothing, a double tap waits half a second
to see whether a third tap follows.
