# FM radio

Works, including **without headphones**. Normally the headphone cable is the
antenna; the Kompakt can use a short internal one instead.

Be realistic about it: a short antenna picks up far fewer stations, and how many
depends entirely on where you are and how close the transmitters are. With
headphones plugged in you get the usual reception.

Open **FM Radio**. The arrows step the frequency, the outer ones jump to the next
station, and the heart saves it. The list button at the top scans for stations
and lists what it finds; tap one to tune to it.
