# FM radio

- Works with or without headphones.
- Without headphones it uses a short internal antenna and finds fewer
  stations.

Open **FM Radio**:

- The inner arrows step the frequency; the outer ones jump to the next station.
- The heart saves the station.
- The list button at the top scans for stations. Tap one to tune to it.
