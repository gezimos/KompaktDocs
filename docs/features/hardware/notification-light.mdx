# Notification light

The LED works and is on out of the box. Its colour is **per app**, set from
Android's own notification light settings rather than a bespoke screen, so
anything that can post a notification can have its own colour.

Settings → **Notifications** → **Notification light**:

<ScreenshotRow>
  <Screenshot src="/img/screens/led-notifications-entry.png" caption="Notification light, under Settings → Notifications." />
  <Screenshot src="/img/screens/led-settings.png" caption="On by default, with colours chosen automatically, and the brightness levels." />
</ScreenshotRow>

- **Brightness levels** (**Normal** and **Do Not Disturb**) dim the light.
  They only dim a light that stays on: a blinking light is always at full
  brightness.
- In a light's settings, **Pulse length and speed** sets how it blinks.
  **Always on** keeps it lit.

Under **Apps**, tap **Add** to give an app its own colour, or tap an app's
colour to change it.
