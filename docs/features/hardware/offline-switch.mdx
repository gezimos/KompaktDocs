# The offline switch

The switch on the side cuts the phone signal and the microphone itself, in
hardware. MuditaOS shows an overlay while it is on; other LineageOS builds
showed nothing at all.

KompaktOS gives it an indicator: flip the switch and a small panel shows which
way it now is.

  <Screenshot src="/img/screens/offline-indicator-online.png" caption="Switched back online." />
  <Screenshot src="/img/screens/offline-indicator-offline.png" caption="Switched offline." />
</ScreenshotRow>

You also choose what else it switches off. Kompakt app → **Offline**:

| Toggle | |
| --- | --- |
| Mobile network | Always cut, by the switch itself |
| Microphone | Always cut, by the switch itself |
| Wi-Fi | Your choice |
| Bluetooth | Your choice |
| Camera access | Your choice |

The first two are cut in hardware, so no setting can change them. The rest are
up to you — cutting Wi-Fi is right if the switch means "offline", wrong if it means
"not listening".

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and camera access are only switched off when you flip the
switch; they are not locked. While offline you can still turn any of them back
on yourself, from Quick Settings or Settings, and it stays on.
