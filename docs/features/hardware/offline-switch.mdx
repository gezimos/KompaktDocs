# The offline switch

The switch on the side cuts the phone signal and the microphone in hardware.
Flip it and a small panel shows which way it now is.

<ScreenshotRow>
  <Screenshot src="/img/screens/offline-indicator-online.png" caption="Switched back online." />
  <Screenshot src="/img/screens/offline-indicator-offline.png" caption="Switched offline." />
</ScreenshotRow>

You also choose what else it switches off. Kompakt app → **Offline**:

| Toggle | |
| --- | --- |
| Mobile network | Always cut, by the switch itself |
| Microphone | Always cut, by the switch itself |
| Wi-Fi | Your choice |
| Bluetooth | Your choice |
| Camera access | Your choice, and a real block |

- No setting can change the first two.
- Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are switched off when you flip the switch, not locked:
  while offline you can turn them back on.
- Camera access, when you choose it, cuts the camera's power while the switch
  is on. Nothing on the phone can turn it back on, not even Android's
  "unblock" prompt.
- To use the camera again, flip the switch or turn Camera access off in the
  Offline tab.
