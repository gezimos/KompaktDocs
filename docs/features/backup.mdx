# Backup

**Seedvault**, LineageOS's backup app, saves your apps and their data, SMS,
call log, contacts and settings, and puts them back when you set up the phone
again.

## Set it up

Settings → **System** → **Backup**.

1. **Backup location**: pick the **SD card** or a USB-C stick. Not the phone's
   own storage, which a reset erases.
2. Write down the **recovery code** it shows. Without it, the backup cannot be
   opened.
3. Turn on **Backup my apps**.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/seedvault-settings.png" caption="Seedvault's backup settings." />

To back up now instead of waiting, open the **⋮** menu and choose to back up
now. **Backup status** shows when the last backup ran.

## Restore

When you set up the phone again, choose to restore from a backup, pick the SD
card or stick, and enter your recovery code. Seedvault then reinstalls your
apps with their data.

<Screenshot src="/img/screens/seedvault-restore.png" caption="Restoring: Seedvault reinstalls your apps." />

## Not in the backup

- **Photos, downloads and other files.** Copy them to your computer:

  ```
  adb pull /sdcard/ kompakt-files/
  ```

- **Apps that refuse to be backed up**, such as WhatsApp, Signal and banking
  apps. Use each app's own backup first. **Backup status** lists them.
